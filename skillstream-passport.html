How to read your passport

  • Said by me You wrote it. It is your own claim.
  • Recorded by the app The app saw it happen, for example a goal with every step ticked.
  • Reviewed by a teacher A teacher checked it. This is coming in a later update.
This passport is not a certificate. Nobody has accredited it, and it does not promise a job, a scholarship or a result.

Add to my passport

Write something you really did or can do, and add a link to show it if you have one.

Share my passport

Only entries you tick "In my share link" can be seen. Visitors see your first name and the first letter of your last name, nothing else. The link stops working after 30 days, and you can switch it off at any time.

Take my passport with me

Download a copy of everything in your passport. It is yours.
