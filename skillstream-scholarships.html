Scholarships

Well-known scholarship programmes, with the official link for each.

Deadlines, amounts and the countries that can apply change every year. Always open the official link and read the current rules before you apply.
How to prepare
  1. Start 6 to 12 months before the deadline.
  2. Read the eligibility rules on the official site first.
  3. Collect your documents: passport, certificates, transcripts and translations if needed.
  4. Check if you need a language test, such as IELTS or TOEFL.
  5. Write your personal statement early, and ask your referees well in advance.
  6. Never pay anyone to be selected. Real scholarships do not sell places.
This list is a starting point and does not include every scholarship. It is not an offer or a guarantee.
